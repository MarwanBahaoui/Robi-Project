# Steal a Ride

Roblox prototype for a vehicle theft, parking, collection, and racing direction.

The current fresh prototype lives in [`fresh_prototype/`](fresh_prototype/). Open [`Steal_a_Ride_Fresh_Prototype.rbxlx`](fresh_prototype/Steal_a_Ride_Fresh_Prototype.rbxlx) in Roblox Studio. The source modules, save-system notes, garage-tier plan, and practice-yard draft are included alongside it.

## Current prototype

The playable prototype includes three van types, theft-triggered guards, arcade driving, manual garage parking, automatic discoveries, duplicate-vans-in-slots, trader sales, five garage tiers, saving with session locks, first-time guidance, and garage navigation.

The next design direction under discussion is racing: stolen vans become race vehicles, sequential race tracks unlock after successful completions, and each van has five race lives. Racing and the free practice yard are design or draft work until explicitly installed and tested in Studio.

See [GAME_CONCEPT.md](GAME_CONCEPT.md) for current decisions and [fresh_prototype/README.md](fresh_prototype/README.md) for prototype controls, limits, and validation status.
